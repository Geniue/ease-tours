## The Real Challenge: U.S. Visa for Egyptians Attending the World Cup

This is the **biggest hurdle** facing most Egyptian fans. The U.S. Embassy in Cairo deals with **lengthy appointment backlogs** that can exceed a full year in some cases. But there's now an official solution designed specifically for World Cup ticket holders.

### What Is FIFA PASS?

**FIFA PASS** (FIFA Priority Appointment Scheduling System) is an official program launched by the **U.S. Department of State** in cooperation with FIFA. It gives **FIFA World Cup 2026 ticket holders** access to **priority interview appointments at U.S. embassies and consulates** around the world.

### How Does FIFA PASS Work?

1. **Buy your tickets directly from FIFA** (not from resale marketplaces).
2. **Opt in to FIFA PASS** through your FIFA.com account.
3. Your ticket data is **automatically synced** with the U.S. visa system.
4. You gain access to **priority interview slots** at the U.S. Embassy in Cairo.

### Key Things to Know About FIFA PASS

- ✅ Completely free — there are no extra fees for FIFA PASS itself.
- ✅ Applications are submitted at the **U.S. Embassy in Cairo** (your country of residence).
- ⚠️ **Does NOT guarantee visa issuance** — you still need to pass the interview.
- ⚠️ Available only to **holders of official FIFA tickets** (resale tickets don't qualify).

## DS-160 Form for U.S. Visa — The Most Critical Step

The **DS-160** is the U.S. nonimmigrant visa application form, and it's the **mandatory online application** for everyone applying for a U.S. visa (B1/B2) from Egypt. Any error on this form can result in visa refusal.

### Information Required in DS-160

| Section | Required Data |
|---|---|
| Personal information | Full name (matching passport) + date of birth + nationality |
| Passport details | Number + issue date + expiry date + place of issue |
| Address | Egyptian residence address + phone + email |
| Work & income | Employer name + job title + monthly salary |
| Purpose of visit | Attending FIFA World Cup 2026 + FIFA ticket reference numbers |
| Trip details | Arrival/departure dates + cities (Seattle or other) + hotel reservations |
| Previous travel | All visas and travel to any country in the last 5 years |
| Family travel | Information for family members travelling with you |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** The form is in English only, and any wrong or incomplete answer requires starting over from scratch. Ease Travel offers **professional DS-160 preparation** with 100% accuracy.

U.S. visa approval for Egyptians depends on **the strength of your file and your confidence during the interview**. Here are the most important tips:

### Required Supporting Documents

- Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date
- Official FIFA World Cup tickets (printed + electronic)
- Provisional flight bookings (no full payment required)
- Confirmed hotel bookings in match cities
- Bank statements for the last 6 months (preferably with at least $5,000 balance)
- Letter from your employer + payroll records
- Proof of property or vehicle ownership in Egypt (ties to home country)
- Enrolment certificate for students or commercial registry for business owners

### Most Common Interview Questions

1. **What is the purpose of your visit to the United States?**
2. **Are you travelling alone or with family?**
3. **How will you fund this trip?**
4. **What is your job and how long have you been employed there?**
5. **Have you travelled abroad before? Do you have prior visas?**
6. **What guarantees your return to Egypt after the tournament?**

> 🎯 **The goal of the interview** is to convince the consular officer that you have **strong ties to Egypt** that guarantee your return, and that you're genuinely travelling to attend the tournament — not to immigrate.

## Frequently Asked Questions About FIFA World Cup 2026 Travel from Egypt

### Does a World Cup ticket exempt me from a U.S. visa?

**No.** An official FIFA ticket is not a substitute for a visa. You must obtain a B1/B2 visa from the U.S. Embassy in Cairo before travelling.

### Can I apply for a U.S. visa without a World Cup ticket?

**Yes**, you can apply as a regular tourist, but without an official ticket you won't benefit from **FIFA PASS** priority, and your appointment may take much longer.

### How much does a U.S. visa cost for Egyptians in 2026?

**$185** as the application fee (Machine Readable Visa fee — MRV), paid to the embassy and **non-refundable** whether your visa is approved or denied.

### Do I also need a Canadian visa to attend the New Zealand match in Vancouver?

**Yes.** Canada has not issued a special World Cup visa, and fans are treated as regular tourists. You'll need a **Temporary Resident Visa (TRV)** to enter Canada — even if you're entering from the United States.

### How long is a U.S. B1/B2 visa valid for Egyptians?

Typically a U.S. visa is issued for **5 years with multiple entries**, but this depends on the consular officer's discretion and the strength of the applicant's file.

### When should I start preparing for FIFA World Cup 2026 travel?

**Now.** As the tournament approaches, embassy appointments fill up and flight + hotel prices climb. The best time to book was 6 months ago — the second-best time is today.

### Are U.S. Embassy appointments in Cairo really available before the tournament?

Yes — particularly through the **FIFA PASS** system or through specialised agencies like Ease Travel. We currently have **appointments available within May 2026**.

### Is travel medical insurance mandatory for a U.S. visa?

Travel insurance **is not mandatory** for a U.S. visa (unlike Schengen visas), but it's **essential** given the extreme cost of healthcare in the United States (insurance starts from $80 for a typical trip).
