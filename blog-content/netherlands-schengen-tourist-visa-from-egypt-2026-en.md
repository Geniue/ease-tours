## What Is a Schengen Visa?

A Schengen visa allows you to enter **27 European countries** with a single visa. The Netherlands is a full Schengen member, meaning a Netherlands tourist visa lets you travel freely across virtually all EU countries for up to **90 days within any 180-day period**.

## Required Documents for Netherlands Tourist Visa from Egypt — 2026

> Every requirement below is taken directly from the **[official VFS Global Netherlands — Egypt checklist](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/netherlands/egypt/english/)**.

---

### 1. 📋 Application Form

- A **completed online and signed** Schengen visa application form — **Original required**
- Must be submitted in person at a VFS Global centre

---

### 2. 🛂 Valid Passport (Original)

Your passport must meet **all** of the following conditions:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | **No older than 10 years** |
| Blank pages | **At least 2 empty visa pages** |
| Validity | **Valid for at least 3 months after your planned departure** from the Schengen area |
| Signature | **Must contain your signature** |

---

### 3. 📸 Passport Photo (Original)

> **Note:** Not required if a live photo will be taken at the VFS Application Centre

The photo must be:
- **Colour** (no black and white)
- **Taken within the last 6 months**
- **3.5 cm × 4.5 cm** in size
- **White or light-coloured background**

---

### 4. 🏠 Proof of Legal Residence in Egypt (Original to show + Copy to submit)

You must prove you are legally residing in the country where you are applying. **One or more** of the following is accepted:

- Valid passport or travel document
- **Residence permit** — must be valid for at least **3 months after leaving** the Schengen area
- Valid visa
- Valid work permit

---

### 5. 💼 Proof of Social or Economic Ties to Egypt (Copy)

This proves you have a reason to return to Egypt. Submit **one or more** documents according to your situation:

#### If you are employed:
- **Letter from your employer** stating: job title + contract duration + salary **or** a certificate of employment
- **Bank statements for the last 3 months**
- **3 most recent pay slips**

#### If you are self-employed:
- **Business permit** or a photocopy of your **income tax return**
- **Bank statements for the last 3 months**

#### If you are a student:
- **Enrolment document** stating: field of study + programme duration

#### Other accepted documents:
- Document proving you are a caregiver in Egypt
- Document proving you own **property or real estate** in Egypt
- Document proving your children are enrolled in school in Egypt

---

### 6. 🏨 Proof of Accommodation in the Netherlands (Copy)

Submit **one** of the following:
- **Confirmed hotel booking or rental agreement** covering the **entire duration of your trip**
- Document proving you own property in the Netherlands

---

### 7. 💰 Proof of Sufficient Financial Means (Copy)

Documents showing you can cover all travel and accommodation costs:

- **Bank statements for the last 3 months**
- **3 most recent pay slips** (if employed)

> This is separate from the "social/economic ties" documents — both must be submitted.

---

### 8. ✈️ Travel Reservation / Itinerary (Copy)

Submit **one or more** of the following:

- **Outward and return flight booking** to/from the Schengen area
  > ⚠️ **A fully paid ticket is NOT required** — a provisional booking is sufficient
- **Booking for an organised tour** in the Netherlands or the Schengen area
- Driving licence + car rental agreement

---

### 9. 🏥 Travel Medical Insurance (Copy)

> **Not required** for holders of diplomatic passports

Your insurance document must **explicitly state**:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Geographic coverage | **All Schengen area countries** |
| Duration | **Entire period of stay** |
| Minimum coverage | **€30,000 (thirty thousand euros)** |
| What is covered | Hospital stay + treatment + emergency care + prescription medication + **repatriation (including in case of death)** |

---

### 10. 👶 Additional Documents for Minors (Under 18)

If the child is travelling **without one or both parents/guardians**:

- **Recent extract of the child's birth certificate**
- **Signed consent form** from the parent(s)/guardian(s) not travelling
- **Signed valid travel document** (e.g. passport) of the non-travelling parent/guardian
- If one parent holds a valid Schengen visa: **colour photocopy** of the biographical data page + the Schengen visa
- If one parent is deceased: **death certificate photocopy**
- If a guardian has been appointed: **photocopy of official proof of guardianship**
- If a court order is in place: **photocopy of the court's permission** for the minor to travel

## ⚠️ Key Rules You Must Know Before Applying

> According to the official requirements on [VFS Global — Netherlands from Egypt](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/netherlands/egypt/english/):

1. **All copies must be in colour** — black and white copies are not accepted
2. If you wish to submit documents not on the checklist, they **must be accepted and uploaded** by the front office
3. If a document is found missing **on the day of submission**, you may return within **2 business days without an appointment** to complete the file
4. After **2 business days**, you must **book a new appointment online**
5. By signing the checklist, the applicant acknowledges that submitting an incomplete file may result in **visa refusal with no fee refund**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I travel to other countries with a Netherlands Schengen visa?
Yes. A Netherlands visa is a Schengen visa, giving you free movement across **27 European countries** throughout its validity.

### Do I need to buy a flight ticket before applying?
No — a **provisional booking** is sufficient. You do not need to pay for the ticket in full before your visa is approved.

### What is the minimum travel insurance coverage required?
**€30,000 (thirty thousand euros)**, covering all Schengen countries for the entire duration of your stay.

### What happens if my documents are incomplete on the day of my appointment?
You can return within **2 business days without a new appointment** to submit the missing documents. After that, a new appointment must be booked online.

### Is a passport photo always required?
No — it is **not required** if the VFS Application Centre will take your live photo during the appointment.
